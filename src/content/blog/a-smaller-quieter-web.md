I miss the feeling of stumbling onto someone’s website and staying for a while. Not because an algorithm predicted I would, but because there was something unmistakably human about it.

A list of favorite books. A half-finished project. A very specific opinion about a very ordinary thing.

## A place, not a platform

A personal site doesn’t have to do much. It needs a readable page, a few good links, and something someone cared enough to put there.

That’s one reason this site is static. Most pages are just HTML and CSS. No account to create, no feed to optimize, no JavaScript to download before you can read a paragraph.

## Make room for the edges

Polish is good. But I don’t want to sand away all the evidence that a person made this.

There’s room for experiments here. There’s room for short notes that don’t become essays. There’s room for an idea that changes next week.

The web is a little more interesting when we make places of our own.
