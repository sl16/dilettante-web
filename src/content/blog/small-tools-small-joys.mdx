Not every project needs to become an app. Sometimes you just want to answer a small question: how long will this take to read?

## One job, done simply

The writing desk below counts words and characters, then estimates reading time. It runs entirely in your browser, with no account, tracking, or server.

<WritingDesk />

Word counts are based on whitespace-separated chunks; they’re a useful approximation, not a language-aware editorial measurement. Reading time assumes 200 words a minute.

## Keep the useful part close

This is also an experiment in putting a tool right next to the idea behind it. A post can be more than a description: it can be a place to try something.

The rest of this site stays static. Only pages that include the tool load its small script. That feels like a good balance: simple by default, interactive when there’s a reason.
