Not every project needs to become an app. Sometimes you just want to answer a small question: how long will this take to read?

## One job, done simply

Word counts based on whitespace-separated chunks are a useful approximation, not a language-aware editorial measurement. Reading time can then be estimated at about 200 words a minute.

## Keep the useful part close

The useful part of a small tool is often the idea behind it: a focused answer to a focused question. Keeping that idea simple makes it easier to understand, adapt, and use wherever it is needed.
